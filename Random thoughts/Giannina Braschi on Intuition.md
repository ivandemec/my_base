> Intuition is the positive energy that envy kills when it refuses to see the rainbow in the sky.

— Giannina Braschi, *United States of Banana* (2011), p. 153

---

**Connection to [[Intuition]]:**

Both thoughts treat intuition as something that acts *before* or *beyond* intellect. Dostoevsky's line — "It takes something more than intelligence to act intelligently" — locates the gap between knowing and doing; Braschi's locates intuition on the affective side of that gap, as energy that envy extinguishes by refusing to see. Where Dostoevsky leaves the "something more" unnamed, Braschi names it: a positive energy, and one that is vulnerable — it can be killed, not by argument, but by a refusal to look.
