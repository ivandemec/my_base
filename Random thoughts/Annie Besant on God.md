> Mysticism is the realisation of God, of the Universal Self. It is attained either as a realisation of God outside the Mystic, or within himself. In the first case, it is usually reached from within a religion, by exceptionally intense love and devotion, accompanied by purity of life, for only "the pure in heart shall see God".

— Annie Besant

---

**Connection to [[Seek God daily]]:**

St Nektarios' instruction is a single move — *seek Him in your heart, not outside it* — and it turns on a distinction Besant makes explicit. She grants that the realisation of God can arrive "as a realisation of God outside the Mystic," reached through the forms and devotion of a religion. But she names the other route as well: the realisation *within himself*. The saint's "not outside it" is not a denial of the first path so much as a claim about where it must finally land — devotion is the door, the heart is the room. And both hold the same condition: "only the pure in heart shall see God." The search is not for a location but for a clarity of the one doing the seeking.
