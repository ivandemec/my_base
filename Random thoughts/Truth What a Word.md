"Truth, What a Word!"

I wonder how there can still be men searching for the truth. Do wise men not yet understand that truth cannot be?

The wise man's resignation springs from inner void, not inner fire. I would rather die of fire than void.

— Emil Cioran, *Selections from 'On the Heights of Despair'*

Source: [Selections from On the Heights of Despair — The Sphere of Ideas](https://thesphereofideas.wordpress.com/emil-cioran-on-the-heights-of-despair-2/)
