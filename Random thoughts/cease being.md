The sky is alive? You are right, cher ami ...

But the earth is dark, cher ami, the coffin thick and the shroud opeque. The eyes of the soul -- to be sure -- if there is a soul and it has eyes! But you see, we are not sure, we can't be sure ...

Men are never convinced of your reasons, of your sincerity, of the seriousness of your sufferings, except by your death. So long as you are alive, your case is doubtful.

In order to cease being a doubtful case, one has to cease being, that's all.

  

~ Albert Camus

from The Fall
