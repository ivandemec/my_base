We, as living beings, must create the opportunity for the dead to perceive us. Let us now consider that we can easily, so to speak, rekindle spiritual life in our souls through a thought. It is already an important positive thought to know that the deceased is there, accessible to us, even after crossing the portal of death, because this is a thought that can never be generated by concern for the sensory-physical world. In our inner life, we must carry the clear conviction: the deceased lives. We know that the dead live. If a person remains in spiritual wisdom, they illuminate themselves, and the dead perceive the light. But if the soul does not absorb spiritual wisdom, it remains in darkness, and the dead cannot perceive it. If the dead can live with us, it depends on what we can offer them. The less a person fills themselves with spiritual wisdom, the less connected they remain to what remains of their life between death and a new birth. In the physical world, souls will become increasingly poor and cold if they do not turn to spiritual life. They only internalize through communion with the spiritual world.
