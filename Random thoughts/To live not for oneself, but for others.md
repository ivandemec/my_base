"The most important thing is to live a moral life, to be kind, to be honest, to be truthful, to be patient, to be courageous, to be unselfish. To live not for oneself, but for others. To help others, to make the world a better place, to leave behind a life that has been useful, not a life that has been wasted. This is the true meaning of life."
