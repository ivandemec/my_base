"Before a man can be a conscious altruist he must first be a conscious egoist" - Mr. Gurdjieff

  

Ego is simply the latin word for I.

  

One of the purposes of the Work is to obtain a Real I.

  

Our problem is we have many egos, many 'I's all partial, mechanical, weak and incomplete.

  

Having a whole completed Ego doesn't seperates me from others, it makes me strong and allows me to serve the Greater Whole for the good of All and Everything.
