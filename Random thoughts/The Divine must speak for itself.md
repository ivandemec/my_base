The Divine must speak for itself—the Divine that begins to speak at a certain point, that lives within the human being as a small, insignificant seed and can develop into infinite greatness.

  

This is what is meant; this is what gave Moses his commission and said: Tell them, “‘I Am Who I Am’ has sent you.” (Exodus 3:14)

  

That which lies like a divine seed in every human soul, that which is enveloped in the physical, etheric, and astral bodies like a point to which we say “I Am,” that which still grows insignificantly above them and which still lives insignificantly within us—we shall not describe this as the least significant part of our being, but as the most important.

  

It is the essential that lives within the human being and that seeks to send Moses by saying, “I am who I am.”

  

****************************

*RUDOLF STEINER* 26 April 1907, Berlin [https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA085/English/SOL/19070426p01.html](https://rsarchive.org/Lectures/GA085/English/SOL/19070426p01.html)
