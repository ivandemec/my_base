"Man, if he wishes to remain at the old materialism—and a great part of mankind does wish so to remain—will inevitably fall into a terrible abyss; he has absolutely no alternative but to go under and become sub-human, he simply cannot maintain himself on the human level. If man would keep on the human level, he must open his senses to the spiritual revelations that have again been made accessible since the end of the 19th century. That is now an absolute necessity."

  

--Rudolf Steiner / World History in the Light of Anthroposophy

and as a Foundation for Knowledge of the Human Spirit : Lecture VI [GA 233]

  

29 December 1923, Dornach
