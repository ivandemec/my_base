> *"We are fulfilled only when we aspire to nothing, when we are impregnated by that nothing to the point of intoxication."*

— Emil Cioran, *On the Heights of Despair* (1934)

> [https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Emil_Cioran](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Emil_Cioran)

---

**Connection to [[Ecstasy]]:**

The original note asks why there shouldn't be "an ecstasy of pure existence" and names metaphysical ecstasy "the ultimate intoxication, ecstatic bliss in the contemplation of essence." This aphorism gives the same rapture a negative inversion: fulfillment arrives not by ascending to the origins of life but by aspiring to nothing — intoxication not with essence but with the void. Cioran's ecstasy of pure existence and his ecstasy of nothingness are twin states, reached by the same radical purification, differing only in what is found at the center: plenitude or abyss.
