# Popper's Falsifiability Principle

## Definition
> A theory must be inherently disprovable to be considered scientific.

## Overview
Philosopher Karl Popper argued that science does not progress by proving things right, but by trying to prove them wrong. If a hypothesis is framed in such a way that no possible empirical observation could ever invalidate it (e.g., "Invisible, untraceable ghosts are everywhere"), it is a pseudoscience, not a scientific theory.

## Relations & Context
* **Related Concepts:** Acts as a systematic filter alongside [[Alder's Razor]] to separate empirical science from unfalsifiable metaphysics.
