There have been at different times and among different people many varying conceptions of the good life.

  

I cannot, therefore, prove that my view of the good life is right. I can only state my view and hope as many as possible will agree. My view is this: that the good life is inspired by love and guided by knowledge.

Niether love without knowledge nor knowledge without love can produce a good life.

  

~ Bertrand Russell
