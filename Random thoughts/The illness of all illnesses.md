# The illness of all illnesses

Today there is an illness that is the illness of all illnesses—we call it “nervousness.” The past did not know it in this way. If the young consciousness of our age cannot convey anything else about the magnitude of the materialistic danger, it should at least tell people about nervousness. Just a century ago, “nervous” referred to someone who could use their muscles. Now a new phenomenon has emerged: a sensitivity that extends to the external senses—that is how far materialism has driven us. The material in the external world has its counterpart in nervousness, and just as the pursuit of the material gains ground, so too will people continue to ruin their nerves to the same extent.

  

Baron Rothschild, who has now passed away in Vienna, was a prime example of this. He left twenty million for a foundation to benefit people with nervous disorders. He amassed this fortune throughout his life as a sick man. He began his life with his nerves in shambles, and that is how he left this world. There is an inseparable link: the chasing and rushing and mere preoccupation with material things are linked to the breakdown of the nervous system. The twenty million is intended for the care of people with nervous disorders—a signature of the ruin of the nervous system. —The occultist knows this deep connection between the spiritual and the outer physical life—but modern man does not yet know it.
