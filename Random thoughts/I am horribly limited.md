What is my life for and what am I going to do with it?

I don't know and I'm afraid.

I can never read all the books I want: I can never be all the people I want and live all the lives I want.

I want to live and feel all the shades, tones, and variations of mental and physical experience possible in my life. And I am horribly limited...

  

~ Sylvia Plath

from The Unabridged Journals
