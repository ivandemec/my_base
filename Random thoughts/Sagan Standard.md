# Sagan Standard

## Definition
> "Extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence."

## Overview
Popularized by astronomer Carl Sagan, this standard acts as a razor to filter out claims that defy established scientific laws or common paradigms. If a claim fundamentally alters our understanding of reality (e.g., alien visitation, time travel), everyday anecdotes are insufficient; the quality of evidence must match the weight of the assertion.
