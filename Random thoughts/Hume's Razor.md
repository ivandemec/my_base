# Hume's Razor

## Definition
> If the cause cannot produce the effect, discard the cause or find a greater one.

## Overview
Formulated by Enlightenment philosopher David Hume, this razor is heavily used when evaluating reports of miracles or extraordinary events. Hume argued that no testimony is sufficient to establish a miracle unless the testimony is of such a kind that its falsehood would be more miraculous than the fact it endeavors to establish.
