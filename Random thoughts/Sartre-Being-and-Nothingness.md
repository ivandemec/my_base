> To believe is to know that one believes, and to know that one believes is no longer to believe..
> Belief is a being which questions its own being, which can realize itself only in its destruction, which can manifest itself to itself only by denying itself. It is a being for which to be is to appear and to appear is to deny itself. To believe is not-to-believe..
> Every belief is a belief that falls short; one never wholly believes what one believes.

— Jean-Paul Sartre, *Being and Nothingness*
