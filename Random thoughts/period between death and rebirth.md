In the period between death and rebirth, all the forces that drive reincarnation, which wish to return, are carried within the soul. These forces are indeed present there. Everything there is directed to ensure that the forces that develop there can only be satisfied with their return to earthly life. Feel that the soul remained imperfect, that it did not develop certain qualities in its last earthly life. Here on Earth, perhaps we are indifferent to perfection or imperfection, but not in the life between death and a new birth. There, irresistible forces compel us to transform imperfection into perfection. We realize that, in many cases, this can only be achieved through suffering and pain, and we know that to achieve perfection, we must assume the sufferings and joys of an earthly life. And so, we enter into a new incarnation with all our forces, so that we can reach the peaks of perfection.
