# Occam's Razor

## Definition
> The simplest explanation with the fewest assumptions is usually correct.

## Overview
Attributed to the medieval theologian William of Ockham, this principle (also called the law of parsimony) states that when presented with competing hypotheses, you should select the one that makes the fewest assumptions. It does not guarantee truth, but it prevents overly complex, unprovable theories from complicating models.

## Relations & Context
* **Counter-part:** [[Hanlon's Razor]] applies this specifically to human behavior by swapping "complexity" with "malice".
