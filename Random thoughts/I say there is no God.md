I say there is no God; that the creation is a myth; that the eternity of the world is certainly no more difficult to believe than the eternity of a spirit ... I say that if everything is the work of a God, then everything should be as perfect as possible; for if everything is not as perfect as possible, this implies either impotence or a will to evil on the part of God.

  

I would wager a hundred to one that out of two men in an argument, the one who loses his temper is the one in the wrong. "You reach for your thunder instead of answering," Menippus said to Jupiter; "that means you are in the wrong."

  

~ Denis Diderot
