# Hitchens's Razor

## Definition
> "What can be asserted without evidence can also be dismissed without evidence."

## Overview
Popularized by journalist Christopher Hitchens in his 2007 book *God Is Not Great*, this razor dictates that the **burden of proof** lies entirely on the person making a claim. If an assertion lacks supporting data, an opponent is not obligated to build a counter-argument to disprove it; it can be rejected immediately.

## Relations & Context
* **Root Origin:** Derived from the Latin proverb *"Quod gratis asseritur, gratis negatur"* ("What is freely asserted is freely denied").
* **Related Concepts:** Works hand-in-hand with the [[Sagan Standard]] regarding evidence requirements.
