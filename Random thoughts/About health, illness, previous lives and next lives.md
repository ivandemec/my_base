About health, illness, previous lives and next lives

Everything we experience externally, without it particularly affecting us internally, has an effect on our astral body in our next incarnation and attracts corresponding feelings, sensations, and peculiarities of thought. If one has made good use of one’s life, observed much, and acquired abundant knowledge, the result is that in the next life the astral body is reborn with special gifts in these areas. Experiences and events, then, are imprinted on the astral body in the next incarnation. But what one feels—pleasure and suffering, the inner experiences of the soul—affects even the etheric body in the next incarnation and creates a lasting inclination within it. Those who experience much joy will have an etheric body with a temperament inclined toward joy. Those who strive to perform many good deeds will, through the feelings developed in the process, have a veritable talent for good deeds in their next life. They will also have a carefully developed conscience and will be morally minded individuals.

  

What the etheric body carries in this life—the enduring character, the predispositions, and so on—manifests in the physical body in the next life, such that, for example, a person who has developed bad inclinations and passions in this life will be born with an unhealthy physical body in the next life. A person, on the other hand, who is in good health and can endure a great deal has developed good qualities in a previous life. Someone who is constantly prone to illness has ingrained bad impulses within themselves. Thus, it is within our power to bring about health or illness for ourselves, insofar as these are rooted in the constitution of the physical body. One need only eradicate all bad tendencies to prepare a good, strong body for the next life.

  

Source: Rudolf Steiner - GA 99 -The Theosophy of the Rosicrucians - Sixth Lecture - 30 May 1907, Munich
