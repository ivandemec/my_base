“It is as pernicious to hate a fellow-being as it is to strike him.”

  

In the spiritual world, a thought is already a deed.

A feeling is a force.

An intention is movement in the moral field.

  

The physical act is only the final density of something that began long before in the soul.

  

Hatred is not harmless simply because it is invisible.

Hatred is already an act.

  

The fist strikes the body.

Hatred strikes the soul.

And the invisible is often more real than the visible.

  

This is why Christ says that to look with lust is already adultery in the heart —

because in the spiritual world, intention is action and desire is already movement of will.

  

Your inner life is never private.

Every thought you cultivate shapes your etheric body.

Every feeling you nourish strengthens a tendency.

Every desire bends your will in a particular direction.

Every intention participates in the architecture of your destiny.

  

Morality does not begin when you finally "do" something.

It begins when you allow something to live within you.

  

You can practice love without speaking a word.

You can betray someone inwardly before ever betraying them outwardly.

You can become courageous long before anyone sees you act courageously.

  

The outer deed is simply the crystallization of an inner deed.

  

This is why Dr Steiner places such extraordinary weight on Love.

  

Love is a way of knowing.

  

Antipathy makes another being opaque.

Sympathy begins to illuminate them.

Love allows their true nature to reveal itself.

  

The world contains far more than we perceive,

but our moral condition determines what we are capable of seeing.

  

You do not simply look at reality.

You meet reality through the condition of your soul.

  

But perception alone is not enough.

  

A truth can appear before you and leave you unchanged.

A person can enter your life and teach you nothing.

A profound experience can happen and remain spiritually useless.

  

Gratitude is what allows an experience to become inner substance.

  

Love opens perception.

Gratitude transforms perception into growth.

  

Your thoughts are not nothing.

Your feelings are not nothing.

Your desires are not nothing.

Your intentions are not nothing.

  

They are the first movements of action.

  

The question is therefore:

  

“What am I doing inside myself?”

  

Because the physical world is only the final layer of a much deeper reality — and every action you have ever taken began as something invisible that you first allowed to live within your soul.

  

If your thoughts are already deeds,

what kind of world are you creating before anyone can see what you do?

Dr Rudolf Steiner
