Franz von Stuck,The Vision of Saint Hubert (Die Vision des heiligen Hubertus), 1890

oil on canvas , 62.5 cm × 53 cm, Museum Villa Stuck, Munich

  

This painting illustrates the medieval legend of Saint Hubert, a 7th-century nobleman and devoted hunter who experienced a divine revelation that caused him to abandon his worldly pursuits and devote his life to God.

  

'Legend holds that on a Good Friday morning, while the faithful were in church, Hubert was hunting in the forest. As he pursued a magnificent stag or hart, the animal turned and Hubert was astounded to see a crucifix floating between its antlers. He heard a voice saying: "Hubert unless thou turnest to the Lord and leadest a holy life, thou shalt quickly go down into Hell." - Wikipedia

  

Unlike traditional interpretations (such as Albrecht Dürer's or Jan Brueghel's prints), von Stuck completely removes the figure of the hunter from the canvas.
