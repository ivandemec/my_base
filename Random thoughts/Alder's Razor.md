# Alder's Razor

## Definition
> "What cannot be settled by experiment is not worth debating."

## Overview
Also known as **Newton's Flaming Laser Sword**, this razor was coined by mathematician Mike Alder. It is a hyper-pragmatic rule designed to slice through purely abstract, metaphysical arguments that have no real-world, measurable impact. If an argument cannot yield an empirical, testable prediction, it is discarded.

## Relations & Context
* **Related Concepts:** Bridges the gap between pure philosophy and physics. It heavily inspired and aligns with [[Popper's Falsifiability Principle]].
