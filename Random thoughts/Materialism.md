Today, we find materialism everywhere, including a form that permeates all practical life, resulting from industrialization. This has made the conditions of business life — and of life in general — much more complex; it has given rise to the rush and frenzy in which humanity is immersed and does not find time to reflect on spiritual science.

Mephistopheles sends Faust to the realm of the Mothers (article) and says: There you will find nothing. — Faust replies: "In your nothing, I hope to find the universe." — But humanity today does not respond as Faust did, because people are trapped in materialism, being possessed by Ahriman.

Despite the fragmentation of modern life in materialism, with the development of the Christian impulse, we must achieve the harmony of the soul and the inner unity of the mind.
