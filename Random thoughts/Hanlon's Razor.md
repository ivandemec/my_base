# Hanlon's Razor

## Definition
> "Never attribute to malice that which is adequately explained by stupidity."

## Overview
This razor serves as a psychological tool to eliminate paranoia and cognitive bias when evaluating the negative actions of others. It suggests that human error, ignorance, or incompetence is a far more common cause of problems than deliberate, malicious intent.

## Relations & Context
* **Related Concepts:** An offshoot of [[Occam's Razor]], favoring the simpler explanation (clumsiness/error) over a complex one (a coordinated plot or malicious agenda).
