We have no reason to hate Ahriman or fear Lucifer
![[Pasted image 20261107092821.png]]
We know very well that we have no occasion either to hate Ahriman or to fear Lucifer, since their powers are inimical only when they are working outside the realm where they belong. [...]

  

If Lucifer were to withdraw entirely from our etheric body, we would have nothing to fire our will. If Ahriman were to withdraw entirely from our etheric body, we would never be able to attain cool thinking. In between stands a region where Lucifer and Ahriman are in conflict. Here they interpenetrate; their activities play into one another. It is the region of feeling. [...]

  

The capacity to have thoughts man owes to the fact that Ahriman can acquire influence over his etheric body. And he has impulses of will because Luciferic forces can acquire influence over his etheric body. These forces are therefore necessary for man, they must needs be present.

  

Source: Rudolf Steiner - GA 158 - The Balance in the World and Man - Lucifer and Ahriman - Lecture Three - 22 November 1914, Dornach
