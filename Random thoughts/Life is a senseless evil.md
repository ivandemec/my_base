Life is a senseless evil, that is certain, I said to myself.

Yet I have lived, and am still living, and all mankind lived and lives.

How is that? Why does it live?

Is it that only I and Schopenhauer are wise enough to understand the senselessness and evil of life?

  

~ Leo Tolstoy

from 'A Confession'
